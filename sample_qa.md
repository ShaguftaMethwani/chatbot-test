### Query 2: Educational Fact (Help Center)
**User:** What exactly is a riskometer in mutual funds?
**Assistant:**
A riskometer is a graphical representation that helps investors understand the level of risk associated with a mutual fund scheme. It categorizes risk into six levels, ranging from Low to Very High, based on the underlying securities in the fund's portfolio.

Last updated from sources: 2026-08-25
**Source Link:** [What is a riskometer](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/general/what-is-a-riskometer)

### Query 3: Statement/Tax Retrieval (Help Center)
**User:** How do I download my capital gains statement for tax filing?
**Assistant:**
To download your capital gains statement, go to your Profile section on the Groww platform and select 'Reports'. Under the 'Tax' section, choose the 'Stocks Capital Gains' report for the required financial year and download it.

Last updated from sources: 2026-08-25
**Source Link:** [How to download capital gains statement](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/taxation/how-to-download-capital-gains-statement)
